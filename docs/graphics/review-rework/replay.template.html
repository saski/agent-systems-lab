Model assumptions and reproducible evidence

24 tasks arrive every two ticks. Four execution slots take eight ticks for initial work and four for a correction. One reviewer takes six ticks per visit. There is no admission limit. FIFO orders attempts by when they become ready for review; priority selects returning corrections first. Neither policy interrupts an active review.

Corrections are prescribed and always succeed. The model has no measured quality, fatigue, varying task value or continuous arrivals. It cannot establish real-team productivity or a general best review policy.
